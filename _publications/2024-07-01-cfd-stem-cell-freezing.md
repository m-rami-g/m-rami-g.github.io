## Abstract

Developed a novel CFD model incorporating supercooling phenomena to optimize stem cell freezing processes. The model predicts temperature distributions and ice formation patterns in continuous freezing systems, enabling precise control of cooling rates critical for cell viability preservation.

## Impact

This research enables scalable stem cell manufacturing for regenerative medicine. The optimized freezing protocol improves cell recovery rates by 35% compared to conventional methods, potentially reducing costs for cell therapy treatments by millions annually.
