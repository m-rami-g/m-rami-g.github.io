## Abstract

Comprehensive CFD model developed for gas-liquid segmented flow in helical microreactors. The model predicts mixing patterns, interfacial mass transfer for multiphase systems with centrifugal force effects in helical pipes.

## Impact

Model enables design of intensified reactors for enhanced mass transfer in the laminar flow regime.
